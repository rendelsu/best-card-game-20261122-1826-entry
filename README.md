**Ember Pact** — a turn-based card battler for make.fun's "Make the best card game" bounty. You duel Cinder, a rival who plays her own hand: spend mana that grows each turn, summon creatures (Ember Imps, Stone Golems, Storm Drakes…), cast Fireballs and Mending Draughts, break through Guards, and reduce the rival to 0 life. Thirteen original cards, a real AI opponent that removes threats, heals when cornered and trades creatures, and a win screen and a loss screen to show for it.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — the game runs entirely from its own static files, no network needed. Click a card to play it, click one of your creatures and then a rival card (or Cinder) to attack, press E to end your turn.
